ROOT CAUSE

  psycopg2.OperationalError:
    FATAL: sorry, too many clients already
    FATAL: remaining connection slots are reserved for SUPERUSER

  Django  CONN_MAX_AGE = 0   (default — new connection per request)
  Postgres max_connections   = 100

  Each VU iteration issues 2 DB-heavy requests (login + task GET).
  At 200+ concurrent VUs the 100-connection ceiling is hit.
  Onset: ~200 VU.  Full saturation: ~300 VU.

  This matches SMP-552 21-day risk register — Risk 2:
  "DB connection pool exhaustion under concurrent middleware load."

NOTE: auth kept in hot loop intentionally

  Auth (POST /auth/login/) is performed every iteration per VU to match
  the real business flow where the middleware fires on authenticated
  requests.  Removing it from the loop would halve DB pressure but
  misrepresent production behaviour.  Thresholds are NOT adjusted.
